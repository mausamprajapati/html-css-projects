Potato Egg Cups

Description

These high-protein egg cups feature a crispy potato base and a fluffy egg-and-cheese filling. Like a cross between a quiche, an omelet, and a muffin, they are perfect for grab-and-go breakfasts throughout the week

Ingredients

  • 12 baby potatoes, unpeeled
  • 2 tablespoons freshly grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 8 large eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup grated Cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons diced red bell pepper
  • 2 tablespoons diced onion
  • 1 tablespoon diced, seeded jalapeño peppers, or more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 3 slices bacon, cooked crisp and chopped

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Generously grease a 12-cup muffin pan or spray with nonstick cooking spray; set aside.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes, cook until tender, 8 to 10 minutes, and drain.
  3. Place a baby potato in each muffin cup. Use a fork or small glass to press each potato to smash it, fully covering the bottom of each cup. Sprinkle each cup with Parmesan cheese.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until light golden brown, about 10 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, add eggs, Cheddar cheese, red bell pepper, onion, jalapeños, parsley, garlic powder, salt, and pepper to a bowl, and whisk until well combined. Fill each cup until 3/4 full with the egg mixture, then sprinkle with bacon.
  6. Bake until egg mixture is set and golden brown, 20 to 25 minutes. Remove from the pan and enjoy warm.
  7. Refrigerate leftovers in a resealable plastic bag or freeze for up to 3 months.
