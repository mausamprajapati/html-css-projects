Dr Pepper Chili

Description

Dr Pepper chili combines ground beef, canned beans, and chipotle peppers with a can of soda to balance the heat. Serve with toppings of your choice, like corn chips, sour cream, and avocado

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 2 poblano peppers, finely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons chili powder, or to taste
  • 2 teaspoons cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo plus 1 tablespoon adobo sauce
  • 1 (12-ounce) can Dr Pepper soda
  • 2 cans mild or fire roasted diced tomatoes and green chiles
  • 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups beef broth or stock
  • 3 cans beans of your choice, e.g. red, pinto and/or kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • toppings of your choice, such as corn chips, onion, sour cream, cilantro and/or avocado (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add beef and cook, crumbling with a spoon until browned and crumbled, about 5 minutes. Drain fat if desired.
  2. Add onion and peppers to the pot and cook, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are beginning to soften, 3 to 5 minutes. Add chili powder, cumin, oregano, salt, pepper, chipotles and sauce, and cook stirring constantly until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  3. Pour in Dr Pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 1 minute. Add in tomatoes, beef broth and beans and stir to combine. Bring to a simmer, cover and reduce heat so chili is simmering. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, for 1 hour
  4. Adjust seasoning if desired and serve with desired toppings.
